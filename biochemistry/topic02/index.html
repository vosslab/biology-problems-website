    pH Using the Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation

    MC BBQ Text Blackboard Ultra ZIP Canvas/ADAPT QTI v1.2
    Click to show pH Using the Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation example problem

    Calculate the pH of the buffer solution.
    The solution contains 687.0 mM of sulfuric acid and 623.0 mM of sodium sulfate.
    (pKa of sulfuric acid = 1.99)
    Henderson-Hasselbalch equation:
    pH = pKa + log10[Base][Acid]
