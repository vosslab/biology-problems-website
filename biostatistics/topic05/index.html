    Errors in Chi-Square Calculations and Hypothesis Decisions

    BBQ Text Blackboard Ultra ZIP Canvas/ADAPT QTI v1.2
    Click to show Errors in Chi-Square Calculations and Hypothesis Decisions example problem
    Table of Chi-Squared (χ²) Critical Values
    Degrees of Freedom Probability
    0.95 0.90 0.75 0.50 0.25 0.10 0.05 0.01
    1 0.00 0.02 0.10 0.45 1.32 2.71 3.84 6.63
    2 0.10 0.21 0.58 1.39 2.77 4.61 5.99 9.21
    3 0.35 0.58 1.21 2.37 4.11 6.25 7.81 11.34
    4 0.71 1.06 1.92 3.36 5.39 7.78 9.49 13.28

    Phenotype Expected Observed Calculation Statistic
     Yellow Round (Y–R–) 90 96 (96-90)²⁄ 96 0.375
     Yellow Wrinkled (Y–rr) 30 25 (25-30)²⁄ 25 1.000
     Green Round (yyR–) 30 31 (31-30)²⁄ 31 0.032
     Green Wrinkled (yyrr) 10 8 (8-10)²⁄ 8 0.500
    (sum) χ² =  1.907

    The final result gives the chi-squared (χ²) test value of 1.91 with 3 degrees of freedom. Consulting the Table of χ² Critical Values and a level of significance α=0.05, we obtain a critical value of 7.81.
    Since the chi-squared value of 1.91 is less than the critical value of 7.81, the null hypothesis has FAILED TO BE REJECTED.

    HA: μ2024 (mu_2024) ≤ μ2014 (mu_2014)
