     Green Wrinkled (yyrr) 10 12 (12-10)²⁄ 10 0.400
    (sum) χ² =  4.511

    The final result gives the chi-squared (χ²) test value of 4.51 with 3 degrees of freedom. Consulting the Table of χ² Critical Values and a level of significance α=0.50, we obtain a critical value of 2.37.
    Since the chi-squared value of 4.51 is greater than the critical value of 2.37, the null hypothesis has BEEN REJECTED.
