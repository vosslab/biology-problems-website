    Genotype Diversity in Hybrid Cross

    Veda and Jay are crossed. Use their genotypes to determine the number of different genotypes possible in their offspring.
    Assume that all genes sort independently and display complete dominance.
    Hint: For each gene pair from the parents, you can get 1, 2, or 3 unique genotypes.
    This is based on the combinations: homozygous x homozygous (1), homozygous x heterozygous (2), and heterozygous x heterozygous (3).
    Considering the principle of independent assortment, how many unique GENOTYPES could be produced in a hybrid cross between the following individuals?
    Use the blank table to organize your work. For each gene, enter the number of possibilities, then multiply across the row to find the total.
    Work in order: (a) and (b) count the different gametes each person can produce; (c) give the Punnett square dimensions (rows × columns) and total number of cells; then count the different offspring outcomes in the remaining row(s). You do not need to draw the full Punnett square.

    Jay genotype AA Bb Cc dd

    Phenotype Diversity in Hybrid Cross

    Aysun and Luke are crossed. Use their genotypes to determine the number of different phenotypes possible in their offspring. An uppercase letter represents a dominant allele; a lowercase letter represents a recessive allele.
    Hint: At each gene, Aa × Aa and Aa × aa can produce two phenotypes. All other crosses produce one phenotype. Multiply the counts across all genes.
    Assume complete dominance and the principle of independent assortment for all genes.
    Given these principles, how many unique PHENOTYPES could result from a hybrid cross between the following individuals?
    Use the blank table to organize your work. For each gene, enter the number of possibilities, then multiply across the row to find the total.
    Work in order: (a) and (b) count the different gametes each person can produce; (c) give the Punnett square dimensions (rows × columns) and total number of cells; then count the different offspring outcomes in the remaining row(s). You do not need to draw the full Punnett square.
    Fill in the white cells and shaded TOTAL cells. Use your final total to select the answer below.

    Genes → A B C D TOTAL
    Aysun genotype AA bb Cc dd  
    Luke genotype Aa bb Cc Dd  
    (a) Aysun gametes          
    (b) Luke gametes
