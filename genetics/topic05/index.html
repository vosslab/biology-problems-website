    Dihybrid Cross Ratios from Epistatic Metabolic Pathways (4 Choices)

    MC BBQ Text Blackboard Ultra ZIP Canvas/ADAPT QTI v1.2
    Click to show Dihybrid Cross Ratios from Epistatic Metabolic Pathways (4 Choices) example problem
    Gene 1
    ✕↓✕ Gene 2
    blue ⟶ white ⟶ redish pink

    A two step metabolic pathway determines the pigment color.
    1. When gene 1 is dominant, the gene expresses an enzyme that inhibits another enzyme that converts the blue precursor pigment into the white pigment.
      When gene 2 is dominant, the gene expresses an enzyme that converts the white pigment into the redish pink pigment.
      When both genes are dominant, the dominant allele of gene 1 inhibits the production of the white color, so it will remain the blue precursor color.
      When both of the genes are homozygous recessive, then the first enzyme is active (not inhibited) and the blue precursor pigment is converted to the white pigment and it will remain the final pigment, as the second enzyme is inactive.


    2. The diagram and description above explain the interaction of two genes. Determine the dihybird cross phenotypic ratio.
