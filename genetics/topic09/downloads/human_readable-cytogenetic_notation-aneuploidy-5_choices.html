1. For human karyotypes, what does the cytogenetic notation 47,+13,XX refer to?
- [*] A. This indicates a female (♀) human karyotype with an added chromosome 13.
- [ ] B. This indicates a female (♀) human karyotype with a partial deletion of chromosome 13.
- [ ] C. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with a partial deletion of chromosome 13.
- [ ] D. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with an extra pair of chromosome 13.
- [ ] E. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with an additional chromosome 13.


2. What does 45,-4,XX signify in a human karyotype?
- [ ] A. This indicates a female (♀) human karyotype with an added copy of chromosome 4.
- [ ] B. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with two extra copies of chromosome 4.
- [ ] C. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with a missing copy of chromosome 4.
- [*] D. This indicates a female (♀) human karyotype lacking copy of chromosome 4.
- [ ] E. This indicates a male (♂) human karyotype with a partial deletion of chromosome 4.
